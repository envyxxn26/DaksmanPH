About Us

Care That Respects Your Privacy

We are a Philippine-based company dedicated to normalize self-care in millions of Filipino men by creating innovative and discreet personal care solutions that improves your confidence and your relationship intimacy. Our mission is to provide high-quality, easy-to-use products that help individuals feel more in control while maintaining comfort, privacy, and trust.

Our product is a single-use wipe formulated to help temporarily reduce sensitivity. It is applied externally before activity and designed for convenience and discretion—simply open one sachet, gently apply to the external area, and allow it to dry completely before engaging in activity. Wash your hands after application. For adults 18+ only. Do not use on irritated skin, discontinue use if irritation occurs, and consult a doctor if needed.
